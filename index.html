About

A fixed point of reference for maritime trade.

In surveying, a datum is the fixed point every other measurement is taken from. Datum sets out to be that reference for maritime supply chains.

We are bringing together information on vessels, ports, terminals and the cargo that moves between them, so the people who plan and run those supply chains can work from the same picture.
